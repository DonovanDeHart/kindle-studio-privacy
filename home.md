# Kindle Studio

**Uses YouTube API Services** · <span style="color:#c00">▶ YouTube</span>

Kindle Studio is a private desktop app used by Donovan DeHart to make and publish videos for his own YouTube channels: Kindle The Spark, Kindle The Cadence and Kindle The Calm. It runs only on the owner's own computer and has no other users.

With the owner's Google sign-in it uploads his finished videos (private or scheduled), sets their thumbnails, adds them to his playlists, and reads his own channels' analytics.
