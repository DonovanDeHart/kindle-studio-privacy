# Kindle Studio – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 29 October 2026*

[Home](home.html) · [Terms of Service](terms.html)

Kindle Studio is a private desktop application made and used only by Donovan DeHart to produce and publish videos for his own YouTube channels: **Kindle The Spark**, **Kindle The Cadence** and **Kindle The Calm**. It is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else and has no other users.

## Use of YouTube API Services
Kindle Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's privacy practices are described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## What data the app accesses
With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in, OAuth), Kindle Studio accesses only the owner's own YouTube channels:
- **Upload and manage:** upload the owner's finished videos, set their thumbnails, and add them to the owner's playlists (`youtube.upload`).
- **Read:** the owner's channel details, uploaded videos and playlists (`youtube.readonly`), to link each upload to its project.
- **Analytics:** the owner's own channel reports such as views and watch time (`yt-analytics.readonly`).

The app does not access other people's channels, comments, subscribers' personal data or any data of other users.

## How the data is used and stored
- All data stays **on the owner's own computer**. There is no server, database or cloud service run by Kindle Studio.
- Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted with Windows DPAPI and are never shared.
- Analytics reports are cached locally for up to 6 hours and refreshed from YouTube on request.
- No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or combined with other data sources.

## Deleting data and revoking access
Access can be revoked at any time at [Google Account → Third-party connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections). Choosing **Disconnect** in the app deletes its stored tokens. Locally cached data is deleted by removing the app's data folders; cached YouTube data is not kept longer than 30 days.
